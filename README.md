# Hi, I'm Liz

I work in growth operations and explore how AI can improve customer insight, go-to-market decisions, and early-stage commercialization.

My work spans mature products and early-stage projects. I look for demand signals, talk to users, examine product behavior, and test why people try, stay, pay—or don't.

AI helps me widen the search, challenge assumptions, and make sense of scattered evidence. I don't treat it as the final decision-maker.

## Current questions

- How can public behavior reveal real—not assumed—customer demand?
- Where does AI improve business judgment, and where does it create false confidence?
- How should an early-stage product move from interest to repeated use and payment?
